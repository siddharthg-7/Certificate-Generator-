# Quick Setup Guide - Certificate Text Placement for College Clubs

## For Users Who Want to Place Name in Specific Location

### Step 1: Identify Where You Want the Name
Open your certificate template (`certificates.png`) and identify where the name should appear.

---

## Common Scenarios

### Scenario A: "Name in the middle of a sentence"
**Example:** "This is to certify that **[NAME]** has successfully completed..."

**Configuration:**
```json
"y_range": [0.40, 0.50]
```

### Scenario B: "Name below a header"
**Configuration:**
```json
"y_range": [0.35, 0.45]
```

### Scenario C: "Name above a signature line"
**Configuration:**
```json
"y_range": [0.55, 0.68]
```
